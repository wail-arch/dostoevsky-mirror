## Quote Safety

- Raw Project Gutenberg text can be used for candidate extraction.
- Candidate quotes remain unverified until manually checked against the source text and location.
- Do not promote extracted quote candidates directly into production.
- Do not use random quote websites or quote aggregators as source material.
